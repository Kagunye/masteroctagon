Who we are

Equipment specialists and engineers, one accountable team.

Master Octagon is a privately owned company specialising in petroleum equipment and related engineering services. We operate within the oil and gas downstream sector, supporting retail fuel stations, terminals and industrial fuel operations across East Africa.

Our team brings together equipment specialists, mechanical and electrical engineers and certified field technicians. We work with fuel marketers, independent station owners, depot operators and industrial users to deliver facilities that are safe, accurate and built to last.
